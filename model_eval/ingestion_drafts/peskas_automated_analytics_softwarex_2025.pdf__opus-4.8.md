# Peskas

## Summary

This document is the SoftwareX 2025 (volume 29, article 102028) "Original Software Publication" titled "Peskas: Automated analytics for small-scale, data-deficient fisheries," authored by Lorenzo Longobardi, Villiam Sozinho, Hamza Altarturi, E. Fernando Cagua, and Alexander Tilley, affiliated with WorldFish (Batu Maung, Penang, Malaysia) and the School of Global Development at the University of East Anglia (Norwich, UK). The paper presents Peskas as a low-cost software solution designed to address the logistical, financial, and capacity challenges of gathering and interpreting data in the small-scale fisheries sector. It establishes Peskas as a template workflow that runs from data ingestion and analysis through to a decision dashboard, intended to be adaptable to different contexts and needs.

## Key concepts

- **Small-scale fisheries importance**: The paper states that small-scale fisheries account for almost 90% of global fisheries employment and are responsible for landing more than 40% of the world's fish catch, while noting their importance to livelihoods and food and nutrition security in Least Developed Countries.
- **Data-deficiency problem**: The authors frame the challenge of small-scale fisheries data as arising from a diverse, dispersed, and informal sector that poses logistical, financial, and capacity barriers to data collection and interpretation.
- **Peskas as a workflow**: Peskas is described as providing a template workflow spanning data ingestion, analysis, and a decision dashboard, designed as a low-cost and adaptable solution.
- **Thematic scope**: The paper's stated keywords identify Peskas as addressing near-real-time analytics, small-scale fisheries, dashboard delivery, stock assessment, and IUU (illegal, unreported, and unregulated) fishing.
- **Software components**: The metadata identifies two distinct released components of Peskas — the Peskas Timor portal (peskas.timor.portal v1.0.0) and the Peskas Timor data pipeline (peskas.timor.data.pipeline v3.1.0) — released under the Apache License.
