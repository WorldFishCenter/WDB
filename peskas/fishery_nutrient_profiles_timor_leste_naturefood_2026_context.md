# Context: fishery_nutrient_profiles_timor_leste_naturefood_2026.pdf

## Summary
Nature Food article (2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43016-026-01313-4) presenting an
analytical framework and predictive model of fishery nutrient profiles in typical
multi-species, multi-gear small-scale fisheries. Using six years of Timor-Leste catch
data (collected through Peskas), it models how fishing method, habitat, vessel type,
and season shape the yield of nutrients of public-health significance — offering
practical, data-driven guidance for nutrition-sensitive fisheries management.

## Key concepts / entities
- Nutrition-sensitive fisheries management (NSFM); ecosystem approach to fisheries
- Fishing method and habitat as strong predictors of catch nutritional profile
- Micronutrients / nutrient yield; "blue foods"; diets in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs)
- Complementary management pathways — different fishing-strategy combinations can reach similar nutritional outcomes while balancing ecological, economic, and wellbeing goals
- Geography: Timor-Leste; 6 years of catch data
- Authors: Lorenzo Longobardi, Gianna Bonis-Profumo, Hamza Altarturi, Jessica Bogard, Joctan Dos Reis Lopes, Jeppe Kolding, Villiam Sozinho, Alexander Tilley
