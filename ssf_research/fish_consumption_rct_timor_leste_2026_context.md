# Context: fish_consumption_rct_timor_leste_2026.pdf

## Summary
PLoS One registered-report article (21(2):e0340861, published 19 Feb 2026): "A supply
and demand intervention increased fish consumption among rural women: A randomized,
controlled trial." A cluster-randomized 2×2 factorial trial in inland Timor-Leste
evaluating nearshore fish-aggregating devices (FADs) and social behaviour change (SBC)
interventions on household fish purchasing and consumption.

## Key concepts / entities
- Malnutrition & child stunting in Timor-Leste; diets low in nutrient-rich foods
- Supply-side intervention: nearshore fish-aggregating devices (FADs) to raise pelagic catch
- Demand-side intervention: social behaviour change (SBC)
- Cluster-randomized 2×2 factorial controlled trial (trial reg. NCT04729829)
- Key result: combined FAD+SBC nearly doubled household fish purchasing (PR 1.90) and women >4× more likely to report eating fish (PR 4.17); neither FAD nor SBC alone was significant — integrated food-system approach needed
- Small island developing states; nutrition-sensitive interventions
- Authors: Alexander Tilley, Kendra A. Byrd, Hamza Altarturi, Gianna Bonis-Profumo, Joctan Dos Reis Lopes, Mario Gomes, Katherine Klumpyan, Lorenzo Longobardi, Kelvin Mashisia Shikuku (WorldFish and partners)
