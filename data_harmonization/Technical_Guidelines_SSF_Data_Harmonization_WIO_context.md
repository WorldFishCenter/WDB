# Context: Technical_Guidelines_SSF_Data_Harmonization_WIO.docx

## Summary
Draft technical guidelines (Version 1.0, April 2026) for harmonizing small-scale
fisheries (SSF) data collection, management, and reporting across institutions in the
Western Indian Ocean (WIO) region. It is a practical roadmap for governments, research
institutions, NGOs, and regional bodies to produce interoperable, comparable, and
policy-relevant fisheries datasets.

## Key concepts / entities
- Western Indian Ocean (WIO) region; small-scale fisheries (SSF)
- "Minimum common denominator" — a core set of consistently defined variables every institution provides
- Four candidate thematic domains: (1) spatial & temporal reference, (2) fishing effort, (3) catch composition, (4) socio-economic & environmental context
- Phase I participatory diagnostic survey of current practices (distributed via an Airtable form); Phase II minimum variable set; standardized sampling methodologies; electronic data-collection tool requirements; FAO ontologies/classification (ASFIS, GAUL administrative layers)
- Integrated Data Harmonization Framework (IDHF; Tungpantong et al., 2021): Diagnostic → Design/Standardization → Implementation/Sustainability
- Scoping assessment of 26 institutions across 10 WIO countries (catch/effort 88%, vessel/gear 84%, socio-economic 48%; only 28% externally compatible; financial sustainability the top barrier at 68%)
- Regional bodies & platforms: Nairobi Convention, SWIOFC, WGDS; CGIAR harmonization strategy
