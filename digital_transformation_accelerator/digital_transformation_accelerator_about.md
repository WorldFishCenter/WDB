# Digital Transformation Accelerator (DTA)

The **Digital Transformation Accelerator** is CGIAR's cross-cutting programme (2025–2030) for
embedding data, digital innovation, and AI across food, land, and water systems research. In the
WorldFish Digital Brain it is the **umbrella initiative** that WorldFish's digital-data packages —
starting with PondCube — are delivered under.

## Aim

Make CGIAR research data and tools **FAIR-by-design and AI-ready**, and move co-developed digital
innovations from research into use — raising the digital-readiness of Programs and Accelerators across
the system.

## Scope (current state)

- **Four Areas of Work:** Data Ecosystem (FAIR / AI-ready data), Action Lab (co-developed
  innovations), Digital Futures (emerging technologies), and Enabling Environment (policy and
  governance), operating across 13 CGIAR Centers including WorldFish.
- **Data Ecosystem** anchors the FAIR / AI-ready agenda — the Carob harmonization framework and the
  GARDIAN platform — alongside AgriLLM (an open-source agricultural LLM) and the CGIAR AI Hub.
- **Asia Digital Hub at WorldFish (Penang)** — the first regional digital-agriculture node in Asia,
  with product areas spanning genetic resource management, water management, and livestock systems.
- WorldFish's contribution under the Accelerator runs through **PondCube**, which moves
  genetic-improvement data into a FAIR-aligned cloud pipeline.
