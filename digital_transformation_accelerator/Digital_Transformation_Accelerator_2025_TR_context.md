# Context: Digital_Transformation_Accelerator_2025_TR.pdf

## Summary
CGIAR's **Digital Transformation Accelerator — Annual Technical Report 2025** (© 2026 CGIAR System
Organization, CC BY 4.0). The Accelerator (director Ram Dhulipala; 2025–2030; US$24.2M in 2025) is
CGIAR's cross-cutting engine for embedding data, digital innovation, and AI across food, land, and
water systems research. It operates through four Areas of Work — **Data Ecosystem** (FAIR and
AI-ready data), **Action Lab** (co-developed innovations), **Digital Futures** (emerging
technologies), and **Enabling Environment** (policy and governance) — across 13 CGIAR Centers,
including WorldFish. This report is the umbrella-programme context for WorldFish's **PondCube**
package of work.

## Key concepts / entities
- Digital Transformation Accelerator (CGIAR), 2025–2030; four Areas of Work (Data Ecosystem, Action Lab, Digital Futures, Enabling Environment)
- Data Ecosystem AoW: FAIR / AI-ready agricultural data, the Carob harmonization framework, the GARDIAN platform
- AgriLLM (open-source agricultural LLM) and the CGIAR AI Hub (Abu Dhabi)
- **Asia Digital Hub established at WorldFish, Penang** — first regional digital-agriculture node in Asia; product areas include genetic resource management, water management, and livestock systems
- FAIR-by-design, AI-readiness, and CGIAR digital-readiness indicators for Programs and Accelerators
