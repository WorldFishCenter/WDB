## Summary
Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems methods article (9:1689512, published 10 Dec
2025; doi 10.3389/fsufs.2025.1689512): "How much is too much information? Testing the
effects of digital feedback on fisher behavior and governance in coastal small-scale
fisheries." It tests how providing digital feedback/information to fishers influences
their behavior and local fisheries governance.
