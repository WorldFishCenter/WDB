# Context: WIOMSA_harmonization_OCT2025.pdf

## Summary
Presentation slide deck (October 2025, WIOMSA context) by Lorenzo Longobardi:
"Data Harmonization — A Collaborative Approach to Unified Fisheries Data in East
Africa." It is the conference-talk companion to the written Technical Guidelines,
making the case for harmonizing SSF data across East Africa / the WIO region.

## Key concepts / entities
- Data collection heterogeneity across five dimensions: information collected; paper vs. digital forms; terminology/variable heterogeneity; species lists & taxonomic resolution; fishery information
- Why harmonization — tangible benefits: stronger visibility & influence (feeds national statistics and regional policy), better fundraising leverage (donors demand comparable evidence), faster insights / less data-cleaning burden, credibility & trust alongside government data
- Regional platforms: Nairobi Convention, SWIOFC
- Geography: East Africa / Western Indian Ocean
